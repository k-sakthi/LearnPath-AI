# LearnPath AI 📘🤖

## 🚀 Project Overview
**LearnPath AI** is an AI-powered exam performance analyzer and concept gap finder designed to help students understand their weak areas after tests and exams.

Instead of revising every topic blindly, students can use LearnPath AI to identify exactly where they are losing marks, which concepts need improvement, and what to focus on next for smarter and more efficient preparation.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
Students often receive only marks or grades after exams, but they do not get detailed insight into:

- Which exact concepts they are weak in
- Which topics they consistently make mistakes in
- What they should revise first
- How to improve efficiently instead of revising everything

As a result, students spend time revising topics they already know and may ignore their actual weak areas.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
**LearnPath AI** solves this problem by analyzing student exam performance and generating intelligent feedback.

The system helps students:

- Upload or enter exam/test results
- Identify weak concepts and low-scoring areas
- Detect patterns in mistakes
- Get AI-powered personalized feedback
- Receive focused improvement suggestions
- Save time by studying only what matters most

This makes exam preparation more targeted, personalized, and effective.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The main objectives of LearnPath AI are:

- To make exam revision smarter and more focused
- To help students discover concept gaps quickly
- To reduce time wasted on broad and inefficient revision
- To provide personalized academic feedback using AI
- To improve student performance through targeted recommendations

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 📊 **Exam Performance Analysis**  
  Analyze marks, scores, or test results to understand performance patterns.

- 🧠 **Weak Concept Detection**  
  Identify specific subjects, chapters, or concepts where the student is struggling.

- 📉 **Gap Finder System**  
  Find learning gaps between current performance and expected understanding.

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Feedback**  
  Generate personalized suggestions for improvement using AI.

- 🎯 **Targeted Revision Suggestions**  
  Recommend what to revise first instead of revising everything.

- 📝 **Focused Study Support**  
  Help students prepare more efficiently for future tests and exams.

- 🌐 **Simple Web-Based Interface**  
  Easy-to-use frontend for quick interaction and accessibility.

## ⚙️ How It Works
### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. The student enters or uploads exam/test performance data.
2. The system reads and organizes the marks or result information.
3. LearnPath AI analyzes the student’s performance.
4. Weak subjects or concepts are identified.
5. The AI engine generates personalized feedback.
6. The student receives:
   - Weak area detection
   - Concept gap insights
   - Improvement suggestions
   - Focused revision recommendations
